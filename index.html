About Us

A culinary journey to Asia without leaving Camberwell

A family café with an Asian soul

At CAFE DE JUNCTION we believe that food is more than nutrition: it's an experience that brings people together. We started as a small family business with the mission of bringing the authentic flavours of Asia to the vibrant neighbourhood of Camberwell Junction.

Our specialty is sweet and sour pork with fried rice and egg, a dish that has won over locals and visitors alike. But you'll also find a wide variety of rice dishes, noodles, starters and drinks prepared with fresh ingredients and traditional recipes.

Located in a high-traffic commercial area, we are the perfect spot for a quick lunch, a casual dinner or a get-together with friends. Our warm and welcoming atmosphere will make you feel right at home.

📍 Prime location: In the heart of Camberwell Junction, steps away from shops, offices and public transport.
